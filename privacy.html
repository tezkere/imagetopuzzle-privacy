Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how the Image To Puzzle app ("the app", package com.games.jigsawpuzzle) handles your information. Contact: info@standalonelab.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: an anonymous identifier created by Firebase Authentication when you start playing. If you sign in with Google, your Google account ID, display name and email address.
  • Game data: puzzle scores, completion times, move counts, XP, level, badges, daily and weekly challenge progress, and leaderboard entries (display name, score, level, country), stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and basic device information (device model, OS version) collected by Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads, and to personalize ads where you consent.
  • Photos you choose: images you select to make puzzles are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

How we use it

To run the game, save your progress, show leaderboards, fix crashes, and display ads.

Advertising and consent

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Profile > Ad privacy options. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed by service providers acting for us: Google (Firebase, AdMob). Leaderboard entries are visible to other players.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and associated data in the app under Profile > Delete account, or by emailing info@standalonelab.com from the address linked to your account. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, which applies access rules to each user's data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.

Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete your Image To Puzzle account and associated data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Image To Puzzle and go to Profile > Delete account.
  2. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm deletion.

Deleting your account removes your account and associated game data, including saved progress and leaderboard information.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email info@standalonelab.com from the email address linked to your account. State that you want your Image To Puzzle account deleted. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
